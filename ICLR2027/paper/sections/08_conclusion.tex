\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} uses alternative contexts as query-level evidence for a fixed
predictor. Consensus weighting identifies mutually supported predictions,
while the gate controls how far the output moves from Base. Across controlled
benchmarks and natural-shift evaluations, this design improves prediction
stability for both foundation and conventional tabular predictors without
backbone retraining.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
The current implementation evaluates multiple candidate contexts for each
query. Adaptive pruning could further reduce one-shot latency beyond the caching
gains reported here, while evaluation under evolving natural shifts would
extend the empirical scope.
