For tabular foundation models (TFMs) pretrained across tasks, inference
depends on both the query and its labeled Context. Alternative Contexts make
inference configurable, but can also destabilize predictions under Context
perturbations and distribution shifts. We propose \textbf{\method{}}, a
query-adaptive ensemble built on this interface. It requires no additional
backbone training or parameter updates. For each query, \method{} constructs multiple
Contexts and aggregates their outputs by confidence and cross-Context agreement.
It modifies Base only when alternative predictions support one another and Base
confidence is low. Across OpenML benchmarks spanning five Context perturbations
and seven distribution shifts, fully synthetic settings, and natural shifts,
\method{} limits performance degradation for multiple foundation and
conventional predictors while largely preserving clean performance. Caching
reusable Context computations further provides a nearly sixfold speedup for
repeated inference.
